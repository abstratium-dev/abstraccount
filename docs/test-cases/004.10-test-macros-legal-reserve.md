# Test Case 004.10: Test Macros - LegalReserveAllocation

**Feature:** Macro System - LegalReserveAllocation Macro  
**Date:** 2026-04-06  

## Preconditions

See [004.1-test-macros.md](./004.1-test-macros.md) for general macro testing preconditions.

Additionally:
- Account 2979 Annual profit or loss must exist (created in test 001)
- Account 2950 Legal reserves must exist (created in test 001)
- Test 004.8 and 004.9 must have been run

## Test Objective

Verify that the LegalReserveAllocation macro correctly creates a transaction to allocate a portion of annual profit to legal reserves, as required by Swiss corporate law (CO Art. 671-671a).

The test will validate:
- LegalReserveAllocation macro parameter form with 3 parameters
- Default value resolution for `{year}-12-31` placeholder
- Transaction creation that transfers profit to legal reserves
- Automatic YearEnd:LegalReserve tag

**Note:** In this test, the company has a net loss, so in practice no legal reserve allocation would be required. However, we test the macro mechanics with a small amount to verify it works correctly.

## Notes

- This macro is **MANDATORY for Swiss Sàrl** under CO Art. 671-671a
- 5% of annual profit must be allocated to legal reserves until reserves reach 20% of share capital
- For CHF 2,000 share capital (test amount), the target would be CHF 400 in legal reserves
- In this test, we allocate CHF 10.00 to verify the macro mechanics
- In practice, this allocation is only done when there is a profit, not a loss
- The allocation moves profit from the distributable account (2979) to the restricted reserve account (2950)
- Once allocated to legal reserves, the funds cannot be distributed to shareholders
